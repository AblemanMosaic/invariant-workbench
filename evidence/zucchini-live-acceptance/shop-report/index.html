Coverage is evidence that a rule observed its requirement being tested. Assertions not tied to a requirement are outcome steps or data table cells that no business rule claims as coverage; Statistics summarizes them. Execution results are shown separately. Missing coverage is derived from declarations; it is not automatically a violation. Execution status comes from the selected CTest JUnit file.
